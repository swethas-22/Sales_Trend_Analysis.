## Data Source Note
This analysis utilizes a simulated e-commerce order table generated with NumPy (`np.random.default_rng(42)`)[cite: 4, 5, 6]. It simulates 2,000 transactions across 300 active customers over the year 2025 across four product categories (`Fashion`, `Electronics`, `Home`, and `Books`), avoiding the exposure of private customer data.

---

## Business Questions
1. **Revenue Trends:** Which months generate the highest and lowest revenues, and what does the month-over-month growth rate look like[cite: 5]?
2. **Category Performance:** Which product categories drive the largest share of total revenue and order volume[cite: 4, 5]?
3. **Customer Concentration (Pareto Analysis):** What percentage of revenue is generated by the top 10% and top 20% of customers[cite: 5]?
4. **Actionable Recommendations:** What concrete commercial actions should leadership take based on these insights[cite: 4, 5]?

---

## Key Analysis Steps
* **Data Inspection:** Verified 2,000 rows with no missing values, correct data formats, and appropriate distribution across order dates and customer IDs[cite: 5, 6].
* **Date Feature Engineering:** Extracted `month`, `quarter`, and `weekday` features from `order_date`[cite: 4, 5, 6].
* **Trend Analysis:** Aggregated monthly sales, computed month-over-month percentage changes with `pct_change()`, and plotted a line chart highlighting the peak and lowest revenue months[cite: 5, 6].
* **Category Breakdown:** Grouped data by category for total sum, mean order value, and volume, followed by a month-by-category pivot table[cite: 4, 5, 6].
* **Pareto Concentration:** Evaluated spend per customer and calculated the revenue contribution of top customer deciles[cite: 4, 5, 6].

# One-Page Business Brief

### 1. Context
This project evaluates retail transactional performance across 2,000 orders to inform marketing strategy, inventory allocation, and customer retention programs for the upcoming fiscal cycle[cite: 4, 5, 6].

### 2. Key Findings
* **Category Dominance:** `Fashion` (~35% of orders) and `Electronics` (~30% of orders) account for the majority of company revenue[cite: 6]. `Home` (~20%) and `Books` (~15%) represent smaller shares[cite: 6].
* **Revenue Volatility:** Monthly revenue tracks seasonal trends with identifiable high and low periods across 2025[cite: 5, 6].
* **Pareto Effect:** Customer spend is concentrated among repeat buyers, with the top 10% of customers generating a significant share of overall revenue[cite: 4, 5, 6].

### 3. Recommendations
1. **VIP Loyalty Program:** Launch a dedicated rewards tier for the top 10% of customers to prevent churn and protect core revenue[cite: 5, 6].
2. **Targeted Inventory Allocation:** Prioritize advertising budget and supply chain stock toward `Fashion` and `Electronics`[cite: 6].
3. **Counter-Cyclical Promotions:** Offer promotional bundles and cross-category discounts during slower months to stabilize month-over-month cash flow[cite: 5].

### 4. Next Steps
* Provide the top-tier customer segment list to the marketing and CRM team[cite: 4, 5].
* Review category performance trends with the merchandising team for seasonal inventory planning[cite: 4, 5].
* Monitor repeat order frequency and MoM revenue growth post-loyalty launch[cite: 5].
